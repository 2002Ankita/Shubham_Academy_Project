## 3. How Tarabai Park Campus Operates3. How Tarabai Park Campus Operates3. How Tarabai Park Campus Operates

Tarabai Park operates on a streamlined, direct academic schedule:
1. Admission & Batches: When a student joins Tarabai Park, the counselor selects their target batch directly (11th PCM, 11th PCB, 11th PCMB, 12th PCM, 12th PCB, or 12th PCMB). No session shift selection is needed.
2. Classroom Attendance: Teachers take attendance for Lecture 1 (Morning) and Lecture 2 (Afternoon). The system shows real student names and calculates attendance percentage instantly.
3. Book Distribution: The campus has its own physical locker. Books handed over to Tarabai Park students are deducted directly from the Tarabai Park stock. Handover slips show the Tarabai Park campus stamp.
4. Fee Collection: All tuition payments made at Tarabai Park are tagged to this branch, and receipts are branded for Tarabai Park.Tarabai Park operates on a streamlined, direct academic schedule:

## 4. How Mangalvar Peth Campus Operates4. How Mangalvar Peth Campus Operates4. How Mangalvar Peth Campus Operates

Mangalvar Peth operates on a high-capacity, dual-shift schedule to accommodate large student cohorts:
1. Shift Selection (Morning vs. Afternoon/Evening): When admitting a student or viewing attendance at Mangalvar Peth, the staff first picks the Session (☀️ Morning Session or 🌙 Evening Session), followed by the Academic Batch (11th/12th PCM, PCB, PCMB).
2. Dual-Shift Attendance: Students attending the Morning shift have their Lecture 1 & Lecture 2 recorded under the Morning schedule. Evening shift students are tracked separately, preventing any confusion between morning and afternoon batches.
3. Mangalvar Peth Book Locker: The locker holds dedicated physical stock for 11th and 12th Theory and CET books. When books arrive at Mangalvar Peth, they are added directly to this campus's inventory.

## 5. Campus Privacy: Zero Confusion, Zero Data Mixing5. Campus Privacy: Zero Confusion, Zero Data Mixing5. Campus Privacy: Zero Confusion, Zero Data Mixing

A major rule in the software is complete data isolation between the two campuses:
• If the receptionist at Mangalvar Peth logs in, they only see Mangalvar Peth students, attendance, fee collections, and books. They CANNOT see, edit, or delete any student or fee from Tarabai Park.
• If the receptionist at Tarabai Park logs in, they only see Tarabai Park students, attendance, fee collections, and books.
• Teachers assigned to Mangalvar Peth only see classes and students of Mangalvar Peth.

## 6. The Director's Master View (Super Admin)6. The Director's Master View (Super Admin)6. The Director's Master View (Super Admin)

The Managing Director / Academy Owner holds the master key to both branches with special controls:
1. Top Campus Switcher: At the top of every dashboard and report, the Director has three buttons:
   • [🏢 All Campuses]: Displays combined student count, total academy revenue, and overall attendance across both branches.
   • [📍 Tarabai Park]: Instantly filters the entire screen to show only Tarabai Park numbers.
   • [📍 Mangalvar Peth]: Instantly filters the screen to show only Mangalvar Peth numbers.
2. Centralized Fee Approvals: If a student at either branch requests a scholarship discount (e.g. ₹5,000 concession), the request appears in the Director's approval box. The Director approves or rejects it with one click.
3. Cross-Branch Teacher Salaries: The Director can view and approve faculty salary slips for teachers who teach at either or both campuses based on their teaching hours and daily activity logs.The Managing Director / Academy Owner holds the master key to both branches with special controls:
